## Order checklist

### 1. Footings / concrete
- [ ] Concrete — **20 bags** (confirm 80 lb vs 60 lb before ordering)
- [ ] ~~Sonotubes~~ — skipped per J
- [ ] ~~Post hole digger~~ — already owned

**Sanity check on 20 bags** (80 lb bag ≈ 0.6 cu ft mixed):
| Hole size | Net per hole (6x6 post in hole) | Bags/hole | 20 bags covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12" dia × 24" deep | ≈ 1.1 cu ft | ≈ 2 | ≈ 10 footings |
| 12" dia × 36" deep | ≈ 1.6 cu ft | ≈ 2.7 | ≈ 7 footings |

Louisville-area frost depth is 24", so at code depth 20 bags supports roughly **8–10 footings**. If the layout has more posts than that, bump the count.
