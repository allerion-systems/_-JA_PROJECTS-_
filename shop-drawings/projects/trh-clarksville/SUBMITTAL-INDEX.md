## Defects found on a full read of all 65 pages

These matter and are carried onto the shop drawing's OPEN ITEMS block.

1. **Tab 9 is a one-page sales flyer with zero dimensions** — no throat size, no
   height, no flange, no gauge, no model number, no net free area, no quantity.
2. **The Tab 9 cut sheet is for a STANDING SEAM vent.** It is titled *"Proper
   Installation of a Vent on a Standing Seam Metal Roof System"* and says the
   vent *"sits on TOP of the standing seam rather than on the flat section of
   the panel."* The TOC and tab divider call it 5V. **No 5V vent literature was
   submitted.**
3. **No project roof slope anywhere in 65 pages** — only Metal Sales' generic
   3:12 minimum.
4. **No metal-roof penetration detail.** Metal Sales manual p.17 cross-references
   "ROOF PENETRATION (SEE PAGE 25)" but only manual pages 17–19 are in the binder.
5. **No title block anywhere in the binder** — no established drawing convention
   on this job.
6. **No trim girths or profile dimensions** for any shop-formed metal.
7. **Downspout conflict:** R&B specifies 4"×4"; the attached Metal Sales manual
   details 4" × 3-1/2".
8. **UL construction number conflict:** p.35 says Const. #435; p.58 says #579 & #453.
9. **Wrong address on the Duro-Last takeoff** (p.23 reads "Louisville, KY").
