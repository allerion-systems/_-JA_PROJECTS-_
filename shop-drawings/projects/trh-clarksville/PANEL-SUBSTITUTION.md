submittal matches:

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|

| Gauge / material | **26 ga, Acrylic-Coated Galvalume** (AZ55 per ASTM A792), colour code 41, non-painted |
| Coverage / rib | **24" net, 1/2" rib**, major ribs 12" o.c. |
| Structure | 50 ksi min yield, 0.78 psf, 3:12 min slope, solid deck |
| Panel length | 45'-0" — **the published maximum** |
| Quantity | 364 panels · 3,843'-4" · 7,686.7 SF incl. waste |
| Approvals | UL 2218 Cl.4 · UL 790 Cl.A · UL 263 · **UL 580 Cl.90 Const. #579 & #453** · Miami-Dade NOA 23-0222.06 · TDI RC-160 · 2023 FBC |

**This is not a proprietary standing seam.** It is the most commodity
configuration of the most commodity exposed-fastener profile there is:
unpainted Galvalume, so there is no paint batch, no colour match and no minimum
colour run. That is the one genuinely favourable fact about this substitution.

## Recommendation, in order

### 1. Union Corrugating 5V — the strongest play by a wide margin

- **QXO already sells it.** QXO — your supplier of record — lists *Union
  Corrugating 26 Gauge × 24" 5V Crimp* as a catalogue SKU. Same supplier, same
  PO, same credit line, same delivery.
- **Same corporate parent as Metal Sales.** Cornerstone Building Brands
  acquired Union Corrugating in Dec 2021 and Metal Sales in Sept 2025. Hard for
  an architect to call a sister brand under the same parent "not equal."
- **Identical UL listing.** Union's installation guide states verbatim
  *"UL 580 Uplift UL Class 90 CONSTRUCTION #579"* — **the same construction
  number cited in your submittal.** Plus UL 2218 Cl.4, Dade NOA 07-1114.01,
  TDI #119.
- **Specs:** 24" coverage, 1/2" rib, 26 ga unpainted Galvalume Plus, 3:12 min.
- **Plants:** Tipp City OH and Morrison TN — both single-day truck runs from
  Louisville. *(Road distances are estimates, not sourced.)*

> **⚠️ The one real risk.** Union's sidelap is **not** the Metal Sales double-V.
> Their guide labels it *"'U' over 'V'" with an "Antisiphoning Channel."*
> Field appearance is identical, **but a Union panel may not lap correctly
> against a Metal Sales panel already on the roof.** If any Metal Sales 5V is
> already installed and the two would interleave on the same plane, **get a
> physical lap mock-up before ordering.** If the remaining work is whole roof
> planes, this is a non-issue.

### 2. McElroy Metal 5V — best regional footprint, one blocker

Plant at **Lewisport, KY**; service centres **Indianapolis** and **Nashville**.
24" coverage, 1/2" rib, 12" o.c., 26 ga Galvalume Plus, 3:12 min, panels to 50'
(covers your 45'-0" runs).

> **⚠️ Blocker:** McElroy's 5V page lists UL 263, UL 790, UL 2218 Cl.4, FBC,
> Miami-Dade and TDI — **but not UL 580.** Your submittal requires UL 580
> Class 90. **First question to McElroy: "do you have a UL 580 Class 90
> construction listing for 5V over 5/8" plywood?"** If not, this is dead.
> Also confirm whether Lewisport actually rolls 5V — its own page describes a
> service centre stocking flat sheet and trim, with no panel profiles listed.

### 3. Best Buy Metals 5V-Crimp — best-documented approvals, worst freight

Publishes the full stack matching your submittal: UL 2218 Cl.4, UL 790 Cl.A,
UL 263, **UL 580 Cl.90**, TDI, FBC, Miami-Dade. 24" coverage, 1/2" rib, 26 ga
standard, **AZ55 per ASTM A792** — exactly your substrate. Panel lengths
5'–45'. 45-year warranty on unpainted Galvalume.
**Nearest location Knoxville TN (~250 mi).**

### 4. MBCI 5V Crimp — sister brand, gauge gap

24" coverage, 1/2" rib, 12" o.c. **But 29 ga standard, 26 ga only optional**,
and approvals are not published on the product page. Confirm both before
spending time.

### ❌ Rule out: Central States

Closest plants of anyone (Scottsville KY, Michigan City IN) — but their own 5V
page states **"This panel does not have any documented certified testing"** and
it is **29 gauge only.** It cannot meet UL 580 / Miami-Dade / TDI. Don't spend
time here.

### ❌ Also ruled out

**ABC** — sister brand, but no plant in KY/IN/OH/TN. **Fabral** — Texas
production, no regional plant. *(Note: a search result attributing Miami-Dade
NOA 23-0222.06 to Fabral is a search-engine conflation — that NOA is Metal
Sales'. Do not cite it.)* **Gulf Coast, Everlast, Reed's, Wheeling, Ideal** —
no regional presence found.

## Getting it approved past Buffalo Construction

This is a **Substitution for Cause** (unavailability within Contract Time), not
for convenience. That distinction matters: most specs prohibit substitutions
for convenience outright but permit them for cause. Use **CSI Form 13.1A**.

**The one document you cannot submit without** — per MasterSpec 01 25 00
¶1.4.A.2.j:

> "If specified product or method of construction cannot be provided within the
> Contract Time, include **letter from manufacturer, on manufacturer's
> letterhead, stating date of receipt of purchase order, lack of availability,
> or delays in delivery.**"

Get that from Metal Sales via QXO. Without it this reads as a substitution for
convenience, which is likely prohibited.

**Then the comparison package:**

1. **Annotated copy of the spec section** showing every deviation (¶1.4.A.2.c),
   with a line-by-line table: 24" coverage · 1/2" rib · 12" o.c. · 26 ga ·
   50 ksi · 0.78 psf · Galvalume AZ55 ASTM A792 · 3:12 min · solid deck ·
   45'-0" max.
2. **Matching test data, one for one:** UL 580 Cl.90 (name the construction
   number — Union's #579 is a literal match), UL 790 Cl.A, UL 263, UL 2218
   Cl.4, Miami-Dade NOA, TDI, 2023 FBC.
   > Do **not** introduce ASTM E1592 / E1680 / E1646 / E331. Those are the
   > standing-seam battery and are not in your submittal — you would be
   > volunteering a test the substitute must pass that the original never did.
3. **ICC-ES research report** (¶1.4.A.2.i). Metal Sales holds ESR-2385.
4. **Warranty match.** Yours is unpainted Galvalume, so this is a
   substrate/perforation warranty, not a 30-yr PVDF finish warranty.
5. **Physical sample.** Get this Metal Sales sentence in front of the architect
   early: *"Differential appearance of Acrylic Coated Galvalume® roofing
   materials is not a cause for rejection."* It protects you on sheen and
   spangle mismatch between two manufacturers' Galvalume.
6. Schedule comparison showing recovery of Contract Time, cost proposal,
   certification of compliance, and waiver of additional time/payment if the
   substitute fails (¶¶j–m).
7. List of similar completed installations with references (¶g).

**Timing:** submit not later than 14 calendar days before the time required for
related submittal preparation and review. The A/E then has 14 calendar days to
accept or reject. Acceptance comes by Change Order.
