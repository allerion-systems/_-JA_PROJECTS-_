## The skill-set map — what Allerion can credibly bid

Joey's log is 40 projects across eight employers. Roofing is the deepest lane
(23 projects) but far from the only one. Ranked by how winnable each lane is for
a new small business:

1. **Repair & alteration of federal buildings (PSC Z2, NAICS 236220)** — the
   single biggest volume of federal *small* construction: office renovations,
   door/window/flooring/painting packages, minor repairs, $25K–$250K simplified-
   acquisition jobs. Direct proof: B3173 office modernization at NSA Crane, Birch
   Bayh courthouse design-build. **This is the primary lane, not roofing.**
2. **Roofing (238160)** — still the calling card. Federal roofs (VA, Job Corps,
   USDA, BOP, National Guard armories) plus the state/local roofs he already
   knows how to win (JCPS, MSD, corrections).
3. **Elevator/conveyance modernization (238290)** — VA runs a constant national
   program of these; few small contractors chase them; Joey has priced one at a
   VAMC with reality-capture data. Niche = margin.
4. **Water/wastewater facility buildings** — MSD Guthrie WQTC and Central
   Maintenance experience maps to USACE civil-works buildings and municipal
   plant work across KY/IN.
5. **Estimating / preconstruction consulting (5416xx)** — sellable *tomorrow*,
   no bonding, no mobilization: estimating for other small GCs chasing federal
   work, plus 3D-scan existing-conditions documentation (541370) for owners
   and A/Es. This is the bridge revenue while construction awards ramp.
6. **Industrial & distillery maintenance construction (private)** — Nucor, Lilly,
   Diageo, Brown-Forman, Maker's Mark, Bardstown Bourbon, Rabbit Hole. Every
   bourbon campus in Kentucky knows his work. Private negotiated work funds the
   federal ramp without bid cycles.

## Saved-search recipes (do once, pipeline fills itself)

On **SAM.gov → Search → Contract Opportunities**, build and **save** each search
with daily email notifications (requires being logged in):

1. **Bread & butter:** Status *Active* · NAICS *236220, 238160, 238290* · Place of
   performance *KY, IN, TN, OH* · Set-aside *Total Small Business*.
2. **Sources sought only:** same filters, Notice type *Sources Sought* — respond to
   every one that fits, even if you won't bid; responses create the set-asides and
   put Allerion on CO call lists.
3. **Elevator niche:** keyword *"elevator modernization"* · org *Veterans Affairs* ·
   nationwide. Low competition; drive-radius doesn't matter for pricing-stage work.
4. **Job Corps roofs:** keyword *"Job Corps"* · NAICS *238160* · nationwide.
5. **SBA SubNet** (subnet.sba.gov) — subcontracting postings by primes; filter KY/IN.
6. **DSBS check:** after updating the SBA profile (playbook, Week 1), search
   yourself in DSBS the way a CO would — NAICS 236220 + KY — and make sure the
   capability narrative reads like the capability statement.
