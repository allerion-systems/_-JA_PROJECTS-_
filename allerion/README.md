## What's in this folder

- **`launch-playbook.md`** — the exit plan: what to do in the next 30/60/90 days,
  the bonding/cash-flow constraints that decide what you can bid, and the math for
  when it's safe to quit the day job.
- **`capability-statement.md`** — draft content for the one-page capability statement
  every contracting officer and prime will ask for. Turn it into a branded PDF.
- **`target-opportunities.md`** — the skill-set map (what Allerion can credibly bid
  beyond roofing), live leads found 2026-08-26, and the saved-search recipes that keep
  the pipeline filling itself.

## Ground rules baked into these docs

1. **Company past performance is zero; Joey's isn't.** Everything the company claims
   must be framed as the principal's experience delivered at prior firms — that's
   honest, and under FAR 15.305 a new entrant gets a *neutral* past-performance
   rating, not a failing one.
2. **Bonding is the gate, not the bid.** Federal construction over $150K requires
   Miller Act performance + payment bonds. Until surety is in place, the biddable
   universe is <$150K jobs, service/consulting work, and sub work — the playbook
   works inside that gate on purpose.
3. **Don't quit until the backlog says so.** The playbook defines the trigger.
