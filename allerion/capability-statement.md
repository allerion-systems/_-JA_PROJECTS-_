  correctional, and healthcare campuses (badging, escorts, infection control,

  scoping and pricing for federal healthcare facilities.
- **Reality-capture estimating** — 3D scan (Polycam/LiDAR) existing-conditions
  models used to price renovation work against field reality instead of legacy
  record drawings; reduces post-award RFIs and change orders.

  facility rules: phased outages in occupied buildings, ICRA-style containment,
