# Launch Playbook — Allerion Technologies LLC

*The plan for turning an active SAM.gov registration into enough awarded backlog to
leave the day job. Written 2026-08-26, six days after activation.*

## The honest picture

- **Assets:** Active SAM registration (UEI KHLLHCKASEH7 / CAGE 23WX9), 40 projects of
  personal past performance worth ~$61.6M, direct VA/GSA/DoD facility experience,
  LSSBB, reality-capture estimating skill, and standing relationships with at least
  four regional GCs and every major bourbon campus in Kentucky.
- **Gaps:** Zero company past performance, no bonding line, no company cash flow,
  one person's spare hours.
- **Consequence:** The first-year game is *small direct awards + sub work + consulting*,
  not $3.8M primes. The $3.8M jobs come in year 2–3, after CPARS ratings exist.

## Week 1 — make the company findable and credible

1. **Verify the SAM record** at sam.gov: entity search "Allerion Technologies" —
   confirm NAICS list, size metrics, and that "purpose of registration" includes
   *All Awards*. Add NAICS 236220 / 238160 / 238290 / 236210 if missing (an
   incomplete NAICS list hides you from CO market research).
2. **Complete the SBA Supplemental / DSBS profile** (prompted from SAM, managed at
   the SBA side): capabilities narrative, keywords, bonding levels, geographic area.
   COs *actually search DSBS* when justifying set-asides — an empty profile is a
   lost bid you never saw.
3. **Check certification eligibility — claim every one that's true:**
   - Small business: automatic via SAM Reps & Certs (size standards: 236220 = $45M
     avg receipts — trivially yes).
   - **HUBZone:** check the home/office address on the SBA HUBZone map. Parts of
     Louisville qualify; if the address is in one, this is a massive edge.
   - **SDVOSB/VOSB:** only if veteran status applies — certify via SBA VetCert.
   - **8(a):** if personally eligible, start the application now; it takes months
     and is worth years of sole-source access.
   - **Kentucky Small/Minority Business certification + Louisville Metro** supplier
     diversity programs for the state/local lane.
4. **Register with the Kentucky APEX Accelerator** (formerly PTAC — free, funded
   for exactly this): 1:1 counseling, bid matching, proposal reviews. Louisville
   office. This is the highest-ROI phone call of the month.
5. **Company basics that COs notice:** domain + email (jallee9544@gmail.com on a
   capability statement reads hobbyist — allerion.tech or alleriontechnologies.com),
   one-page website, Google Business profile, dedicated bank account, GL insurance
   quote, and workers-comp arrangement (KY requires it from the first employee;
   sole-member exemption is possible but many owners opt in — primes will ask for
   the certificate either way).
6. **Finish the capability statement** (`capability-statement.md` → branded PDF).

## Weeks 2–4 — open the two revenue lanes that need no bonding

- **Consulting lane:** Offer estimating/precon and 3D-scan existing-conditions
  services to the GCs already in the phone: Valiant, Kelley, Cordia, Agile, BMWC.
  A new LLC invoicing $5–15K/mo of estimating work is cash flow, a company track
  record, and zero conflict with future prime bids. *(Mind the current employer's
  no-moonlighting/non-compete terms — read the handbook before the first invoice.)*
- **Sub lane:** Register Allerion in the subcontractor portals of the 5–10 primes
  that win small-business work at Fort Knox, Crane, Fort Campbell, and the VAMCs
  (start with the USACE Louisville MATOC awardees when announced — see
  `target-opportunities.md`). Send the capability statement with a two-line email:
  who you are, what packages you self-perform or price.
- **Set up the pipeline:** Build the six saved searches in
  `target-opportunities.md`. Respond to **every** fitting sources-sought notice —
  that's how set-asides get created and how COs learn the name.
- **Register on the local portals now** (MSD Bonfire, JCPS, KY eProcurement, UK
  Purchasing) — state/local jobs have no Miller Act bonds below KY's thresholds,
  faster award cycles, and Joey's name is already on winning past bids.

## Months 2–3 — first bids

- **Bid band: $25K–$150K federal jobs** (above micro-purchase, below the $150K
  Miller Act bond line, inside the $250K simplified-acquisition small-business
  reserve). Roof repairs, interior renovation packages, door/flooring/painting,
  small elevator repairs. Expect to bid 10–20 to win 1–3; that's normal.
- **Start the SBA Surety Bond Guarantee application** in parallel (sba.gov →
  Surety Bonds): SBA guarantees up to 90% to the surety for new firms — this is
  how a company with no balance sheet gets its first $500K single / $1M aggregate
  line. The day it lands, the biddable band jumps to $250K+.
- **Price with the differentiator:** scan-based existing conditions on every
  renovation bid; it shows up in technical evaluation on best-value jobs.
- **WAWF/PIEE registration** at first DoD award (invoicing); **prompt-payment
  reality:** feds pay Net 30 from a proper invoice — model 60 days of float per job.

## The exit trigger — when to actually quit

Leave the W-2 when **all three** are true:

1. **Backlog:** signed awards + subcontracts + consulting agreements ≥ **6 months of
   personal expenses + company overhead**, with at least one award in performance.
2. **Runway:** 6 months of personal cash *separate from* company working capital
   (jobs eat cash for 30–60 days before the government pays).
3. **Insurance/benefits solved:** health coverage lined up (marketplace or spouse),
   GL + WC certificates active — primes and COs both ask before NTP.

Rule of thumb from every contractor who's done this: the day job funds the launch;
quitting early to "focus on bidding" just adds panic to the proposals. Target:
**trigger conditions met inside 9–12 months** — SAM renewal (2027-08-10) is a good
deadline to beat.

## Cadence (put on the calendar now)

- **Daily (15 min):** SAM.gov saved-search email triage; follow/dismiss.
- **Weekly (2 hrs):** one sources-sought response *or* one prime intro email; log
  every touch in a simple CRM sheet.
- **Monthly:** APEX Accelerator check-in; pipeline review — kill stale leads, and
  raise the bid band as bonding/cash allow.
- **2027-06-15:** start SAM renewal (due 2027-08-10). Never let it lapse.
