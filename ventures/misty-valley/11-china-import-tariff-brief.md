- Classification anchor: CBP ruling **HQ 083363** puts steel-skinned foam-core panels in
  heading 7308. Complete prefab building **kits** (9406.20) sit at 25% Annex I-B +
  25% 301 List 3 + 2.9% MFN ≈ 52.9% — panels alone are not a "building"; do not stretch
  it without a **binding ruling**.
- **AD/CVD: no order covers finished sandwich panels** (Fabricated Structural Steel from
  China died at the ITC 2/2020, no injury). BUT the CORE orders on coated **coil** run
  ~199% AD + up to ~241% CVD and Commerce has never squarely scoped foam-core panels —
  get a **scope ruling** before committing volume. Commerce opened a Thailand
  transshipment circumvention inquiry 7/6/26 (91 FR 40969): any supplier offering
  "no tariffs via Thailand/Vietnam" is proposing customs fraud that lands on the
  importer — us.
- Mechanics: no SIMA license for fabricated panels; ISF $30–75; broker $150–400/entry;
  continuous bond outgrows the $50k minimum fast at 75% duty.

## Landed economics (Sept 2026 freight)

1×40HQ of 50mm EPS panel @ $9/m² FOB (~1,300 m² effective): goods $11,700 + duty
$8,834 + WC freight ~$7,000 + drayage/broker/PSI ~$1,750 = **~$29,300 → ~$2.09/sf
landed**. Still ~half the cheapest domestic channel (surplus cooler panels
$5.71–7.94/sf) — the tariff does not kill the math; **code compliance does** (no US
listing → ag-exempt / non-occupied / uninsured use only; see 03-product-compliance-risk).
East/Gulf freight ~$9,100–9,800/FEU changes the math materially on thick panels.
