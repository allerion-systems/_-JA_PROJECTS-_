## The four things that kill this

1. **Wiring money before the customs broker's letter.** $32,489, not $21,000.
2. **Selling material that fails the 95% rule.** It reaches Construction, not just
   Supply, and it is the only finding that can damage the ten-year-old business.
3. **Undocumented related-party sales.** Legal when disclosed. Fatal when not.
4. **Running out of working capital at $4M of revenue.** Peak cash need is
   **$499K–$708K** depending on structure. Growth consumes cash faster than
   profit makes it. **This is the constraint, not demand.**

## And the thing nobody will tell you

**East South Central nonresidential starts are down 16.3% year to date while the
national market is up 17.6%.** You are launching into one of the three
worst-performing regions in the country.

**That is an argument for the knowledge business, not against the business.** A
logistics-heavy operation needs density and throughput it will not get in a
shrinking corridor. **A supplier who wins on the takeoff can run at low volume
and follow the work.** Build the one that survives a soft market, because that is
the market you have.
