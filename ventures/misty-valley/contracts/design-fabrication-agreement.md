This Agreement covers structures configured and priced through MVS's configuration model. MVS's Standard Terms of Sale apply except where this Agreement states otherwise.

## 1. Scope of Work

1.1 The scope of MVS's supply is exactly the **bill of materials ("BoM") generated by the priced configuration model** identified above, as attached or referenced in the order confirmation.
1.2 Anything not on the quoted BoM is excluded, including sitework, foundations, utility connections, and finishes not listed.
1.3 The configuration model output (model version, options, and pricing) attached to this Agreement is the definitive statement of scope. Verbal descriptions, renderings, and marketing materials do not modify scope.

## 2. Change Orders

2.1 Any change to the configuration — options, dimensions, materials, delivery scope — must be requested in writing.
2.2 **Every change is repriced by running the revised configuration through the model.** There is no line-item ad hoc pricing; the new model output replaces the prior BoM and price in full.
2.3 A change order is effective only when signed (or electronically accepted) by both parties. Changes after fabrication begins may also incur supplier change fees and schedule extensions, which will appear in the repriced model output.
2.4 If Customer requests a change the model cannot express, MVS may decline it or quote it as a separate agreement.

## 3. Customer Responsibilities

3.1 **Site.** Customer provides a site that is accessible, prepared, graded, and suitable for delivery and set as described in the delivery requirements attached to the order confirmation, including foundation or pad if required.
3.2 **Permits.** **All permits and approvals (building, zoning, floodplain, septic, driveway, and any other) are Customer's responsibility**, unless MVS expressly agrees to obtain a specific permit in a separately signed writing for additional consideration. MVS's price assumes no permit work by MVS.
3.3 **Utility locates.** Customer must arrange utility locates (call 811) before any delivery, set, or anchoring work, and must mark private utilities not covered by the locate service.
3.4 **Decisions.** Customer designates one authorized contact for approvals and change orders.
3.5 Delays caused by Customer's failure under this section may extend the schedule and add documented standby, redelivery, or storage costs.

## 4. Delivery and Set

4.1 Delivery and (where included in the BoM) set/placement are performed at the address on the order confirmation. Delivery dates are estimates.
4.2 Customer warrants site access is adequate for the trucks, cranes, or equipment described in the delivery requirements. MVS may stop work if the site is unsafe or inaccessible; resulting costs are Customer's.
4.3 Title and risk of loss pass at delivery per the Standard Terms of Sale. Where set is included, risk of loss for set operations remains with MVS or its rigging subcontractor until set is complete.
4.4 Punch-list items noted at completion of set will be corrected by MVS or the manufacturer under the warranty pass-through; they do not delay final payment beyond any retention stated in the payment schedule.

## 5. Payment — Authorize/Capture Milestones

5.1 The contract price is payable in the milestones stated on the order confirmation (e.g., deposit at signing; progress at fabrication start; balance at ship-date confirmation or delivery).
5.2 For each milestone, Customer's card is **authorized** through MVS's payment processor (Stripe) when the milestone is invoiced and **captured only when the milestone event is confirmed** (e.g., supplier fabrication start, confirmed ship date). MVS does not hold Customer funds before capture; until capture, funds remain under the card issuer's authorization managed by the processor.
5.3 Expired authorizations may be re-requested; the schedule pauses (without Customer default) while re-authorization is pending, but MVS may suspend supplier releases until payment for the due milestone is captured.
5.4 If Customer cancels after fabrication begins, Customer is responsible for supplier cancellation charges and the non-returnable value of fabricated or special-order items per the Standard Terms of Sale.

## 6. Warranty; Liability

6.1 Warranty is the manufacturer's warranty passed through per the Standard Terms of Sale; MVS provides no independent warranty. Set workmanship performed by MVS's subcontractor carries the subcontractor's stated workmanship warranty, if any, passed through on the same basis.
6.2 The limitation of liability in the Standard Terms of Sale applies to this Agreement.

## 7. Governing Law and Venue

7.1 Kentucky law governs. Exclusive venue: state courts sitting in Hart County, Kentucky (or the federal district encompassing Hart County, where required).
