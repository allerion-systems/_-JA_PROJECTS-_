This is MVS's form for dealer/dropship arrangements in which Supplier ships directly to MVS's customers.

## 1. Relationship and Roles

1.1 MVS is an authorized dealer/reseller of the covered Products. The parties are independent contractors; nothing creates a partnership, agency, franchise, or employment relationship.
1.2 **MVS is the merchant of record** for all sales to MVS customers: MVS sets retail pricing, contracts with the customer, collects payment, and handles sales tax on the retail transaction. Supplier invoices MVS at the agreed dealer price and looks only to MVS for payment.
1.3 Supplier fulfills orders by shipping **directly to the delivery address MVS provides** for each order.

## 2. Fulfillment and Blind Shipping

2.1 **Blind shipping is preferred:** shipments and included paperwork should identify MVS (or be neutral), not Supplier's retail branding or pricing. At minimum, no dealer-cost pricing may appear on anything delivered to the customer.
2.2 Supplier will confirm each order and provide an estimated ship date within ____ business days of MVS's purchase order, and provide tracking on shipment. Confirmed ship dates matter to MVS's payment flow with its customers; Supplier will notify MVS promptly of any slip.
2.3 Supplier will pack adequately for freight and, where applicable, provide delivery requirements (equipment, access) MVS can pass to the customer in advance.

## 3. No Contact with MVS Customers

3.1 Customer identities and addresses provided for fulfillment are MVS confidential information. **Supplier will not solicit, market to, or otherwise contact MVS customers** except as strictly necessary to complete a delivery MVS has arranged (e.g., carrier delivery scheduling), and will not use customer data for any other purpose.
3.2 This restriction survives for ____ years after termination. Warranty service contact initiated by the customer under Supplier's warranty is permitted.

## 4. Product Compliance and Warranty

4.1 **Supplier warrants** that each Product: (a) conforms to its published specifications; (b) complies with applicable laws, safety standards, and certification requirements for sale and use in the United States, and carries the certifications stated in its documentation; (c) is new, free of liens, and non-infringing; and (d) is covered by Supplier's or the manufacturer's written product warranty, which extends to the end customer.
4.2 Supplier will supply current spec sheets, warranty documents, certification evidence, and installation instructions, and keep them current. MVS relies on these in reselling.
4.3 Supplier will honor warranty claims from MVS customers per the written warranty, with MVS assisting in claim submission.

## 5. Indemnification

5.1 Supplier will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless MVS from third-party claims, losses, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from: (a) **product defects**, including design, manufacturing, and warning defects; (b) Supplier's breach of the warranties in Section 4; (c) IP infringement by a Product; and (d) Supplier's negligence or misconduct in fulfillment.
5.2 MVS will indemnify Supplier against claims arising solely from MVS's own misrepresentation of Products beyond Supplier's documentation.
5.3 Supplier will maintain commercial general liability and product liability insurance in commercially reasonable amounts and, on request, name MVS as additional insured and provide certificates.

## 6. Returns, Defects, and Recalls

6.1 Products dead-on-arrival, freight-damaged (where Supplier arranged freight), or non-conforming will be replaced or credited at Supplier's cost, including return freight.
6.2 Supplier will promptly notify MVS of any recall or safety issue and bear recall costs for affected Products.
