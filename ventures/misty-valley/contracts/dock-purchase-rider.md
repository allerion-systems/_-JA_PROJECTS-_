This Rider adds terms specific to floating docks and attaches to the referenced agreement, whose terms otherwise apply.

## 1. Shoreline-Use Permit

1.1 Placement of a dock on regulated waters typically requires a shoreline-use permit or similar approval from the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers ("USACE") and possibly other authorities. **Obtaining and maintaining all such permits is solely the Customer's responsibility.**
1.2 **MVS's assistance is limited to preparing the permit sketch** (a site/dock drawing derived from the configured model, suitable for inclusion in a permit application). MVS does not prepare, file, or prosecute permit applications, does not communicate with USACE on Customer's behalf, and makes no representation that a permit will issue.
1.3 Customer is responsible for confirming permit status before delivery. If Customer schedules delivery or installation without required permits, resulting relocation, removal, or penalty costs are Customer's alone.
1.4 If a permit is denied before fabrication begins, Customer may cancel per the referenced agreement's cancellation terms; special-order/fabricated components already begun remain non-returnable.

## 2. Water Level and Acts of God — Exclusions

2.1 Docks are sold for the water conditions described in the configuration. **MVS has no liability for, and no warranty covers, damage or unusability caused by:** water-level fluctuation (including drawdowns, flood pool changes, and drought), flood, wave action, wake, ice, wind, storm, debris strike, or other acts of God or natural events.
2.2 Customer is responsible for seasonal adjustment, storm preparation, and any relocation or removal required by the waterbody's managing authority.
2.3 Customer is encouraged to insure the dock; MVS does not provide or arrange insurance.

## 3. Anchoring and Warranty Pass-Through

3.1 The dock must be **anchored and installed in accordance with the anchoring plan** included with the configured order (and any manufacturer installation instructions).
3.2 The manufacturer's warranty passed through under the referenced agreement is **conditioned on installation and anchoring per plan**. Deviation from the anchoring plan — including substitution of anchor hardware, spacing changes, or omission of components — voids the warranty pass-through to the extent the manufacturer denies coverage, and MVS has no obligation to cover the gap.
3.3 If installation is performed by Customer or Customer's contractor, Customer should document as-built anchoring (photos, hardware list) to preserve warranty claims.
3.4 MVS provides no warranty of its own on the dock or its installation, consistent with the referenced agreement.

## 4. Delivery and Launch

4.1 Customer provides a launch/assembly area and water access adequate for the delivery method stated on the order confirmation. Risk of loss passes at delivery per the referenced agreement, including for docks delivered to shore for Customer launch.

## 5. Governing Law and Venue

5.1 Kentucky law governs; exclusive venue is the state courts sitting in Hart County, Kentucky (or the federal district encompassing Hart County, where required), per the referenced agreement.
