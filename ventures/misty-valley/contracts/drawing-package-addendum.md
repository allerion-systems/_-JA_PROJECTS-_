## 1. Deliverable

1.1 MVS will produce a drawing package derived from the **priced configuration model** for the Project: plan view, elevations, sections, and the associated IFC data export.
1.2 Drafting begins **after the referenced agreement is signed** and the underlying configuration is locked. Changes to the configuration after drafting begins are handled as change orders and may reprice or restart the drawing work.
1.3 Delivery format: PDF drawing set plus IFC file, delivered electronically.

## 2. Tiers

2.1 **Drafted tier ($450).** Unsealed drawings prepared by MVS drafting staff from the model. Suitable for planning, pricing, and discussion with Customer's builder or officials who accept unsealed drawings.
2.2 **Sealed tier ($1,400).** The drafted package is reviewed, and where appropriate revised, and sealed by an **independent licensed professional engineer engaged through MVS's partner network**. The engineering review and seal are performed solely by the licensed engineer under Kentucky's professional engineering licensure law (KRS 322).
2.3 **MVS provides drafting services only. MVS does not practice or offer engineering.** All engineering judgment, calculations, and the seal itself are the licensed partner engineer's work and responsibility. MVS's role for the sealed tier is coordination and drafting support.
2.4 The partner engineer may require site or configuration information from Customer, and may decline to seal a configuration; in that case Customer may accept the drafted tier (with the fee difference refunded) or cancel the sealed tier for a refund of the sealed-tier fee.

## 3. Fee and Payment

3.1 The tier fee is **billed on order confirmation** of this Addendum, via the payment method and authorize/capture process in the referenced agreement.
3.2 The fee is non-refundable once drafting begins, except as stated in 2.4 or where MVS fails to deliver.

## 4. Intellectual Property and License

4.1 **MVS retains all ownership** of the drawings, IFC data, the underlying configuration model, and all derivatives (for the sealed tier, subject to the partner engineer's rights in the sealed instruments).
4.2 Customer receives a **non-exclusive, non-transferable project-use license**: the right to use the drawings and IFC data for permitting, financing, construction, and maintenance of **this Project at the site identified in the referenced agreement only**.
4.3 The license does not permit reuse on another project or site, resale, publication, or modification for construction of a different structure. Reuse on a different site requires a new package (site conditions differ, and any seal applies only to the stated site).
4.4 If Customer terminates the referenced agreement before completion, the license terminates for construction use but survives for record-keeping.

## 5. Use Limits and Reliance

5.1 Drawings describe the configured structure as modeled. They are not a substitute for site-specific civil, geotechnical, foundation, or utility design unless those items are expressly included in the sealed engineer's scope.
5.2 Only the sealed tier may be represented to any authority as engineer-reviewed. Customer will not represent drafted-tier drawings as sealed or engineered.
5.3 Whether a given jurisdiction accepts the package is the Customer's permitting risk, consistent with the permit allocation in the referenced agreement.
