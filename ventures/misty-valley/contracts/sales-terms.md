2.1 MVS sells many items on a preorder basis: the item is ordered from the manufacturer or supplier after Customer places the order.
2.2 At order placement, Customer's payment card is **authorized** for the order amount through MVS's payment processor (Stripe). The card is **not charged (captured)** until the supplier confirms a ship date for the order.

2.4 If the supplier cannot confirm a ship date within the window stated on the order confirmation (or 30 days if none is stated), MVS will release the authorization or offer Customer the choice to re-authorize and continue waiting.
2.5 If an authorization expires before capture, MVS may request re-authorization; the order is not in default while re-authorization is pending.
