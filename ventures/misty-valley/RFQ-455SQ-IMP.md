## 4. Freight & Lead-Time Reality

- **How IMPs ship:** factory-cut panels in banded bundles on flatbed trucks; panel lengths 8' min to **50' max based on a 48' flatbed trailer** (Vulcan Steel). 45,500 sf will be **multiple flatbed loads** — get panel count/bundle plan and truck count from each quoter. ([Vulcan Steel IMP page](https://www.vulcansteel.com/insulated-metal-panels/))
- **Freight cost ballpark:** national average flatbed spot rate ≈ **$3.65/mile (Aug 2026, DAT)**, up sharply YoY (was ~$2.06–2.07/mile in 2025). Computed examples (rate × distance, per truck): Little Rock AR→Bonnieville (~450 mi) ≈ $1,650/truck; Lewisville TX (~750 mi) ≈ $2,700/truck; DeLand FL (~800 mi) ≈ $2,900/truck. No published per-project IMP freight figures were found — ask each supplier for delivered pricing. ([GlobeNewswire/DAT Apr 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/14/3273584/0/en/DAT-Truckload-freight-rates-hit-two-year-highs-as-diesel-costs-surge.html); [Transport Topics/DAT](https://www.ttnews.com/articles/dat-truckload-spot-rates); [DAT flatbed blog](https://www.dat.com/blog/flatbed-report-steel-output-rises-to-start-2026-signaling-firmer-flatbed-freight-demand))
- **Lead times:** major-brand IMP lead times running **10–16 weeks from order** (Kingspan, Metl-Span, CENTRIA, PermaTherm) as of early 2026 per Terrapin's commercial lead-time tracker; older norms were 2–4 weeks standard / 6–12+ weeks custom; quick-ship programs (PermaStock, American Insulated Panel) can ship stock panels in days-to-weeks. **Lead time may matter more than price — ask on every call.** ([Terrapin lead times 2026](https://terrapincg.com/news/commercial-construction-material-lead-times-2026); [Metal America ordering guide](https://metalamerica.com/how-to-order-custom-metal-roofing-panels-what-contractors-need-to-know/); [PermaStock](https://permatherm.com/permastock/); [American Insulated Panel](https://americaninsulatedpanel.com/cold-storage-resources/unbeatable-lead-times-how-american-insulated-panel-stands-out-in-the-industry/))

## 5. RFQ Checklist — answers Ben needs BEFORE calling

1. **Sandwich IMP or foam-laminated single-skin?** (Photo shows PU-foam sandwich; confirm interior metal facer is wanted — this halves or doubles the price.)
2. **Panel length / cut list** — roof plane dimensions, eave-to-ridge lengths, number of pieces (max ~50' shippable).
3. **Profile & rib height** — high-rib trapezoidal (LS-36 / KingRib / HR3 style) vs standing seam (CFR / RidgeLine / KingSeam)? Match to photo.
4. **Exposed fasteners acceptable?** (1.5" products found are exposed-fastener or KingRib; seamed panels start at 2"–2.5".)
5. **Core thickness/R-value target** — is 1.5" a hard requirement or is R-value the requirement? **If any supplier's minimum is 2"/2.5", is that acceptable?** (Biggest spec fork.)
6. **Exterior gauge** — 26 vs 24 ga; interior facer gauge/finish (26 ga typical).
7. **Color/finish both faces** — exterior color + interior liner (white?); paint system.
8. **Fastening system** — through-fastener vs clips; screw/clip package included in quote?
9. **Trim & flashing package** — ridge, eave, gable/rake, valley, penetrations; closures (inside/outside foam closures at eave/ridge).
10. **Roof slope** — must be ≥ 1/2:12 for the IMP roof systems cited.
11. **Substructure** — purlin spacing/type (drives gauge and span check).
12. **Delivery address + site unloading** (crane/forklift for 40-50' bundles?) and **need-by date**.
13. **Code/insurance requirements** — FM approval, fire rating, UL uplift, energy code R-value?
14. **Quantity confirmation** — is 455 squares net roof area or does it include waste? Who owns overage?
