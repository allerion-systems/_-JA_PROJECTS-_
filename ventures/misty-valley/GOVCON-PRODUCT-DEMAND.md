# GovCon Product Demand — What the Government Actually Buys (MVS Catalog Expansion)

Researched 2026-09-06 via Tango (SAM.gov/USAspending data) + web verification. GovTribe was unavailable (subscription inactive).
All dollar figures are what a cited record actually says. Summary figures are capped samples (first 50 of N matching records), NOT market totals — treat them as "is this real and recurring," not market size.
FY2025 = federal fiscal year Oct 2024–Sep 2025.

## Part 1 — MVS's existing lines: does the government buy them?

### 1. Safety / PPE (PSC 4240, NAICS 423840) — YES, at massive volume, but crowded
- **What's bought:** first aid kits, eye/face protection, fall protection, laser eye protection, goggles — thousands of small orders.
- **Who buys:** DLA (dominant), GSA FAS (Global Supply BPAs), Air Force/Navy/Army base contracting, USCG.
- **Evidence:** 16,709 FY25 contract actions under PSC 4240. Sample of 50 = $2.54M obligated, avg $50.9K. Examples: W50S7J25PA005, $20,000, first aid kits (Army NG, MS); FA486125P0189, $22,328, laser eye protection, SBA set-aside (99 CONS, USAF).
- **Reality check:** ADS Inc. took 25 of the 50 sampled awards; Noble Supply and AbilityOne (Federal Prison Industries) take much of the rest. This is a scale-players' market.
- **MVS verdict: stock it** — but sell it via purchase-card micro-buys and base-level small-biz set-asides, not DLA national contracts.

### 2. Prefab / portable buildings (PSC 5410/5411, NAICS 332311) — YES, strong small-biz fit
- **What's bought:** storage sheds/buildings, vault toilet buildings, fabric shelters, SCIF containers, guard structures.
- **Who buys:** Air Force & Army base contracting, Navy, CBP, Forest Service, plus MICC offices at Fort Campbell/Knox.
- **Evidence:** 392 FY25 actions under PSC 5410; sample of 50 = $19.2M, avg $385K; 25 of 50 SBA set-asides. Examples: FA469025P0069, $39,990, replace ATV storage sheds, Ellsworth AFB, SBA set-aside; 1240LR25F0026, $30,485, vault toilet building (USFS). Live local demand: solicitations W911S226U4264 / W911S226U4265 "Storage Building" (PSC 4235, NAICS 332311, total small business set-aside, Fort Campbell, closed 9/2/2026) — exactly MVS's product.
- **MVS verdict: stock it** — this is MVS's best existing line for federal work; Fort Campbell is already soliciting it as total-SB.

### 3. Shipping containers (PSC 8145 shipping/storage containers) — YES
- **What's bought:** new/one-trip steel containers, climate-controlled containers, specialized transit cases.
- **Who buys:** DLA (28 of 50 sampled), Navy warfare centers, Army, Air Force.
- **Evidence:** 4,757 FY25 actions under PSC 8145; sample = $10.6M. Example: N0016725P1195, $40,040, one 20-ft + one 40-ft new climate-controlled containers delivered to FL/MD, SBA set-aside (NSWC Carderock). Sea Box and Containers Unlimited are repeat winners. Also seen: Fort Knox QRP selling surplus conexes (QRP26-0014) — installations churn containers constantly.
- **MVS verdict: stock it** — plain-container delivery orders in the $20–60K range are winnable for a distributor with yard inventory near I-65.

### 4. Floating docks / waterfront (USACE, USCG, VA, USFS, NPS) — YES, niche and underserved
- **What's bought:** modular floating dock systems, accessible/ADA kayak launches, gangways, dock replacement.
- **Who buys (sample of 50, FY23–26):** USCG (18), Navy (11), US Fish & Wildlife (4), Forest Service (3), NPS (3), VA, State Dept. USACE lake offices (Louisville District runs Nolin River Lake, Rough River Lake, etc. — both ~30 min from Bonnieville) buy recreation-area dock work; no specific recent LRL dock award surfaced in this pass ("not found" — check SAM.gov under W912QR).
- **Evidence:** 79 matching awards FY23–26. Examples: 36C24526P0623, $51,981, adaptive kayak launch + modular floating dock system (VA); 12445526P0037, $97,280, accessible floating dock + kayak launch install, SBA set-aside (USFS); N4008526F0910, $247,900, install L-shaped floating dock (NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic).
- **MVS verdict: stock it** — few vendors, product-plus-light-install scope, and MVS sits between two USACE Louisville District lakes.

### 5. Lumber / building materials (PSC 5510, NAICS 423310) — YES, but DLA-centralized
- **Evidence:** 4,031 FY25 actions under PSC 5510; DLA Troop Support (SPE8E) took 41 of 50 sampled, mostly against existing IDVs. DLA Construction & Equipment is the executive agent for Class IV construction/barrier material and runs standing lumber/plywood programs. Retail-ish exceptions exist: 1240BK25C0002, $25,449, supply and delivery of trail-bridge lumber, SBA set-aside (USFS).
- **MVS verdict: dropship it** — don't chase DLA prime programs yet; take one-off USFS/NPS/base lumber buys opportunistically and register on DLA DIBBS for small RFQs.

### 6. Guard booths — YES, steady replacement demand
- **Evidence:** 109 awards FY23–26 (keyword); sample of 50 = $12.7M, avg $254K. Buyers: GSA Public Buildings Service (19 of 50), State Dept embassies (11), Army. Examples: 47PD5326F0311, $68,000, guard booth replacement, KC federal courthouse; 47PE5226F0248, $45,778, guard booth replacement at FERC. Delta Scientific (a booth OEM MVS could dealer for) won 19SG2026P0593, $115,674.
- **MVS verdict: dropship it** — become a dealer/installer partner; most buys are booth + freight + crane set, ideal dropship-plus-site-crew.

## Part 3 — On-ramps (verified Sep 2026)

**Thresholds (FAR 2.101, effective Oct 1, 2025):**
- **Micro-purchase threshold: $15,000** (raised from $10,000) — government purchase-card buys below this need no competition, no SAM-posted solicitation, just a willing cardholder. This is the real entry point. *Exception: construction work subject to wage-rate requirements has a much lower MPT (~$2,000) — supplies deliveries are fine, installed construction is not.*
- **Simplified acquisition threshold: $350,000** (raised from $250,000) — under SAT, buys are reserved for small business by default and run as quick RFQs (the Fort Campbell storage-building/boiler/chiller pattern above).

**Path:**
1. SAM.gov registration (free, ~2–4 weeks incl. UEI + CAGE). NAICS to claim: 423840, 423310, 332311, 423610; small under all.
2. Check the SBA HUBZone map (maps.certify.sba.gov/hubzone/map) for the Bonnieville/Hart County address — rural KY counties frequently qualify; HUBZone certification would unlock sole-source awards up to ~$4.5M (not verified this pass — confirm on the map).
3. KY state/local: register on Kentucky VSS (eMARS Vendor Self Service, finance.ky.gov/eProcurement) + the KY Small Business CONNECTion database (Cabinet for Economic Development, KY-located small businesses only).
4. DLA DIBBS account for small lumber/container/PPE RFQs (DLA C&E, code SPE8E, is executive agent for Class IV construction/barrier material).
5. GSA MAS: skip for year one. It requires past performance and ~$25K/24-months minimum schedule sales, and everything above shows open-market + set-aside routes work without it. Revisit once federal revenue exists.

## 5-step on-ramp checklist

1. **Register in SAM.gov** (UEI → CAGE → reps & certs; claim NAICS 423840/423310/332311/423610); simultaneously check the SBA HUBZone map for the Bonnieville address and start HUBZone cert if it qualifies.
2. **Register on KY VSS/eMARS + Small Business CONNECTion** for Commonwealth and local-government bids.
3. **Build a purchase-card one-pager/catalog** (PPE, ice melt, mats, fence materials, flagging, small sheds, container rental/sale) and get it in front of Fort Knox and Fort Campbell cardholders and the MICC offices (W9124D Fort Knox, W911S2); every buy ≤ $15K needs no bid.
4. **Set SAM.gov saved searches** for PSC 4235/5410/8145/4240 + "dock," place of performance KY/TN, total-SB set-asides; quote everything under the $350K SAT; open a DLA DIBBS account.
5. **Line up dealer/dropship agreements now** (guard booth OEM e.g. Delta Scientific-class, generator distributor, precast barrier plant, fabric-building OEM, dock-system OEM) so quotes on $50K–$250K set-asides can go out in days, not weeks.
