# Import-direct program (Made-in-China.com class sourcing)

*2026-09-07. How MVS lists and sells imported building units without losing
the tariff math or crossing a customs line. Companion: research/TARIFF-STUDS-CHINA-VS-WORLD.md.*

## The listing rule
Only categories whose FOB price SURVIVES the full China duty stack (~85%:
50% §232 full-value + 25% §301 + 10% §122) and still beats the domestic
equivalent get listed. Today that is expandable/folding container units
(MVS-IM-EXP37 / EXP19 / FOLD20). Steel building kits, metric framing and
most everything else LOSE to domestic after duty — not listed, per our own
research. Re-run the math when rates change.

## The mechanics (why this is not AliExpress dropshipping)
A 5-ton unit is ocean freight with an importer of record — customs entry,
bond, duties. Model: PREORDER AGGREGATION. Units list as preorder; orders
accumulate against a container booking; the booking is placed when it
fills. Customer card authorized at order, captured when the booking is
confirmed with a real ship date.

## Landed-cost pricing discipline (per unit, planning figures)
FOB + duty (broker-confirmed rate) + ocean/inland freight + entry costs,
then the 60% floor on that landed cost. Example EXP37: FOB ~7,500 + ~85%
duty ~6,375 + freight ~3,500 ≈ 17,400 landed → sell 28,500.

## Red lines (unchanged, absolute)
- Broker-confirmed HTS classification and AD/CVD scope screen IN WRITING
  before any booking. Rates move; the broker letter is the number.
- Never DDP "tariffs included" offers with second invoices, friendly HTS
  codes, or third-country routing. Undervaluation / misclassification /
  transshipment is customs fraud. Walk, every time.
- Never copy supplier listing photos or text — our own copy, our own
  images, per the standing image policy.
- Honest habitability: imported units are structures, not certified
  dwellings. Habitable use = county permit path, stated on the page.
  No fabricated certs, ever.

## Supplier vetting checklist (before the first booking)
1. Factory video call + production photos of OUR order class.
2. Sample unit or third-party inspection (SGS-type) before balance payment.
3. Payment: deposit/balance via trade terms — never 100% up front.
4. Spec sheet in writing: panel ratings, electrical standard (must be
   NEC-compatible or sold as unwired), imperial dimensions confirmed.
5. Two references from US buyers who cleared customs with this factory.
