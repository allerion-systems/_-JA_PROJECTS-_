Quality & compliance

The paperwork matters as much as the steel.

Light-gauge steel is easy to get wrong in ways nobody can see on site. Base metal thickness, coating weight, and yield are invisible in a bundle and invisible in a wall — right up until an inspector, an architect, or a fire marshal asks. Here is how we handle it.

Documented to the lot

Mill test reports for the actual production run, filed against your delivery — not a generic type certificate pulled from a folder.

Verified, not assumed

We verify base metal thickness and coating on incoming material rather than taking a certificate at face value. Every standard has a minimum; we check that ours clears it.

Right product for the assembly

Structural and non-structural are different standards, and a rated assembly is a tested assembly. We will tell you when a substitution is not a substitution — including when that costs us the sale.

Traceable

Lot traceability from the coil to the bundle to the job. If a question ever comes back years later, we can tell you exactly what went where.

Product certifications and listings vary by manufacturer and product line. Ask us for the specific evaluation report, listing, or mill documentation covering the material on your job and we will provide it before you buy.
