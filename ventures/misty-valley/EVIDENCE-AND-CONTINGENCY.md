# Evidence Architecture & Contingency Plans

*Directive: everything traceable, iron-clad in court; Ben grows into R&B's main
material sourcer. Those are the same program — the relationship survives scrutiny
only if the records would. Not legal advice; the attorney reviews this framework.*

## 1 · The evidence rules (how records become court-grade)

**Contemporaneous beats reconstructed.** A note written the day of the call outweighs
a memory sworn a year later. Every material conversation gets a same-day written
confirmation: *"Ben — confirming our call today: 455 squares, 1.5-inch, quote due
Tuesday, middle pricing."* Sent by email or text — never left verbal.

**Third-party timestamps beat our own.** Email through Gmail, texts, signed PDFs,
bank records, and USPS/carrier records carry timestamps no party controls. The git
repository is our working record (every quote, disclosure, and decision is committed
with a timestamp); the *legal* record is the email/PDF trail. Rule: any document that
matters gets EMAILED to the counterparty (or at least between Ben and Joey) the day
it's final — that email is the exhibit.

**One numbered file per deal.** Quote #MVS-2026-0001 already exists; every deal keeps
the sequence and a folder holding: customer request (Ben's text/screenshot) → supplier
quotes (PDFs as received) → selection rationale (the comparison workbook, middle-price
rule documented) → our quote as sent → PO → order confirmation → invoices → delivery
documents (signed BOL/photos at the jobsite) → payment records. A deal file that reads
end-to-end IS the proof.

**Money leaves a clean trail.** Every dollar through the business account, never
personal; invoices numbered; KY sales tax collected or the resale certificate filed —
the certificate is part of the deal file.

**The permissions ledger.** Image permissions in `image-permissions/` (already ruled),
supplier terms and dealer agreements as signed PDFs, insurance certificates dated.

**Retention:** nothing deleted. Deal files, texts, and emails kept for 7 years minimum.
The instinct that saves you in court is the same one that runs a clean company.

## 2 · The R&B relationship — the only legal path to "main sourcer"

Plain words: Joey is R&B's chief estimator until October. An employee steering his
employer's purchases to his own company **without written disclosure** is self-dealing
— breach of the duty of loyalty, civil liability, termination for cause, and the end
of the reputation this company runs on. The SAME relationship **with disclosure and
consent** is an ordinary vendor file. The difference is one signed page.

**The gates, absolute until Joey's last R&B day:**
1. The signed disclosure (draft at `contracts/dual-role-disclosure-DRAFT.md`) is in
   Scott's hands and acknowledged BEFORE the first quote is delivered.
2. Ben ↔ Scott is the only commercial channel. Joey gives R&B no purchasing advice on
   any item MVS quotes, recuses himself in writing if asked, and takes no part in
   R&B's evaluation of MVS bids.
3. Arm's-length pricing, documented: the comparison workbook shows how each price was
   built from market bids. R&B gets the same terms any customer of its size would.
4. Zero R&B confidential information (their costs, bids, supplier terms) in MVS files
   — and the deal files prove it, because every input is sourced.
5. After October the gates relax to #4 plus ordinary professional conduct.

**Growth plan (earn it, deal by deal):**
- Deal 1 (455 SQ): flawless — on time, in spec, documented. Nothing sells like the
  first order arriving right.
- Vendor package: Ben delivers R&B a one-page vendor file — W-9, certificate of
  insurance (GET CGL BEFORE FIRST DELIVERY — see contingencies), payment terms,
  Ben's direct line, response-time commitment (quotes back in 48 hours).
- Line card: after deal 1, Ben hands Scott the one-pager of what MVS sources —
  IMP panels in six cores, steel framing, fasteners, safety gear — "one call, one
  invoice, jobsite delivery."
- Cadence: Ben checks in per project cycle, not per week. Suppliers who deliver and
  don't pester become the default phone call. That is what "main sourcer" is.

## 3 · Contingency plans

| Scenario | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Scott declines or reacts badly to the disclosure** | The quote is withdrawn, no hard feelings, in writing. MVS loses one customer and keeps its founder's reputation. R&B becomes a prospect for November. |
| **Joey's R&B employment ends early** | The gates in §2 convert immediately to post-exit rules. Nothing else changes — which is the point of building it clean now. |
| **Supplier fails mid-order (no-ship, bad panels)** | The PO names the spec and ship date; the deal file holds the supplier's confirmation. Remedy ladder: written cure demand → substitute supplier (the two-bidder file means the silver medalist is already priced) → refund to customer per our terms. Never re-spec silently. |
| **Freight damage on delivery** | Photos at load and at jobsite (already SOP), full-value freight insurance on building/panel shipments, claim filed within carrier window; customer made whole first, claim recovered second. |
| **Payment dispute with a customer** | The numbered deal file is the case: quote → acceptance → delivery proof. Terms in `contracts/sales-terms.md` (attorney to finalize venue/late-fee clauses). |
| **A supplier or competitor alleges image/IP misuse** | The permissions ledger answers in one email. Anything without filed permission never published — standing rule. |
| **Dispute between Joey and Ben** | The operating agreement (drafted, attorney pending) governs; until signed, every material decision confirmed by text/email between them — the contemporaneous-record habit is ALSO the partnership's protection. |
| **Insurance gap** | Before the first physical delivery: CGL policy (customer-facing certificates available on request); before any PPE sale: product liability per the pre-mortem counter. Uninsured delivery is a contingency plan with no plan. |
