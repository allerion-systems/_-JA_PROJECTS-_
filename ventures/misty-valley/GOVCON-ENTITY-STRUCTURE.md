## The rules that decide which bids Allerion can honestly win

- **Nonmanufacturer rule (13 CFR 121.406; FAR 52.219-33).** On a SMALL-BUSINESS
  SET-ASIDE supply contract over the simplified threshold, a bidder that didn't make the
  product must: have ≤500 employees, be primarily a dealer/reseller of such products,
  take ownership or possession consistent with industry practice, and supply the product
  of a SMALL U.S. MANUFACTURER — or bid under an SBA class waiver where one exists.
  - **Sheds:** WORKS. The manufacturer is the Hart County shop (small, U.S.). This is
    the strongest lane for Allerion-prime + MVS-supply + Elijah-manufacture.
  - **Conex containers:** PROBLEM to verify before any bid. Containers are
    foreign-manufactured; the NMR and Buy American Act both bite. SBA maintains class
    waivers for products with no small U.S. manufacturer — VERIFY whether shipping
    containers are covered (SBA class waiver list) before bidding any set-aside conex
    RFQ; micro-purchase-level buys are the safer entry meanwhile.
  - **Dock hardware / PPE:** same NMR analysis per manufacturer; favor U.S. small
    manufacturers from the dropship network (FrenchCreek is U.S.-made fall protection —
    check size status per SKU).
- **Micro-purchases (≤$15K, FAR 2.101) and simplified acquisitions** have lighter
  set-aside mechanics — the on-ramp lane while the compliance muscle builds.

## The two risks this structure deepens (say it plainly)

1. **Concentration in Joey's entity (pre-mortem #10).** Allerion already owns the
   platform; making it the govcon prime routes revenue AND the customer-of-record
   relationship through Joey's solo LLC while Ben and Elijah sit in MVS. Efficient now;
   a partnership grievance later unless papered.
2. **Related-party dealing.** Joey controls both sides of the Allerion↔MVS supply

   authorized dealer #001; MVS list-price schedule; blind-ship terms; who carries
   product liability and freight risk on government orders) plus the Allerion↔MVS
   **platform IP agreement** already flagged. Same attorney visit, one package.
