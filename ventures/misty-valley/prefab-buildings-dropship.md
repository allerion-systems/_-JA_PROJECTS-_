## Call first — top 5 for MVS

1. **American Steel Carports** — only supplier with a published number (10% commission), factory delivers AND installs, zero inventory; classic roadside-dealer model.
2. **Old Hickory Buildings** — consignment sheds placed on your lot at no cost, company handles build/RTO financing/delivery; KY/TN home turf on the I-65 corridor.
3. **Esh's Utility Buildings** — KY-based (Burkesville) with an explicit become-a-dealer page and existing lots in E-town/Glasgow; shortest supply line of anyone on this list.
4. **VersaTube (Dealer Builder)** — no fee, no stock, order one kit per sale at wholesale pricing, freight prepaid; TN plant close to Bonnieville.
5. **ShelterLogic** — the one program that says "drop ship" out loud, plus private branding; greenhouses/shelters/carport kits in one account.

Runner-up: Hansen Pole Buildings (dealer discounts + free leads, kits engineered and shipped direct to the customer's jobsite).
