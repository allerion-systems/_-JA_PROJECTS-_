Commodity PPE (hard hats Z89.1, glasses Z87.1) shows up mostly inside GSA/DLA channels and safety-plan clauses, not standalone base RFQs, in this sample.

## 7 · First three bids (most winnable, micro-purchase / simplified-acquisition lanes first)

**Prerequisites first (blocking, do in this order):**
1. **SAM.gov entity registration** → UEI issued in-process, **CAGE code** assigned on activation; complete small-business reps & certs (NAICS 321992, 332311, 423390, 423840, 444180 etc.). Registration now takes weeks — start immediately (the Green River Lake notice itself warns of this).
2. **SBA Dynamic Small Business Search (DSBS) profile** — KY, capability narrative naming sheds/containers/docks/PPE.
3. **PIEE Solicitation-module vendor account** (piee.eb.mil) — without it you cannot even read W911S2/USACE specs; add **WAWF (invoicing)** and **ProjNet** (USACE Q&A) accounts.
4. Kentucky APEX Accelerator (free bid-matching/mentoring) — not mandatory, cheap leverage.
5. Micro-purchase lane: prior research pegs the FAR 2.101 micro-purchase threshold at $15K — sales below it need no bid at all; get the card-friendly catalog in front of Ft. Campbell/Ft. Knox cardholders in parallel with everything below.

**Bid 1 — Wood storage sheds, delivered & set (183EIS001-type RFQs).** Total-SB, PSC 5410/NAICS 321992, spec = exactly what the Hart County Amish shop builds, "delivery and set-up on customer prepared lot" is the whole install scope, and awards run low-five-figures (prior memo: FA469025P0069 $39,990 ATV sheds — re-verify). Respond to sources-sought notices (like 183EIS001) even before the RFQ: it gets MVS on the bidders list and can shape the spec toward stock configs.

**Bid 2 — Conex container delivery orders, plain and lightly modified (FHWA 69056726Q000039 / USCG 16910Q260000003 class).** PSC 8145 had ~4,757 FY25 actions (prior memo); typical order $20–60K; LPTA against a written checklist MVS's one-trip SKUs already meet (WWT, Corten, marine-ply floor, lockbox). Quote = container + lockbox + freight + tilt-bed offload; the required "cut sheets demonstrating all minimum requirements" are a one-time documentation build per SKU. Modified units (man-door, insulation kit, load center, window AC) are the existing MVS-CI kit list assembled by the container upfitter.

**Bid 3 — USACE Louisville District dock work, starting supply-only.** Immediate: Sandy-Lake-style **dock supplies** RFQs (floats, PT boards, poles, hardware — pure catalog, no bonds). Then step up to **Green River Lake W912QR26QA108** (open until 2026-09-21, 30 miles from Bonnieville, lowest-price award): only if MVS can partner a bonded local marine/dock installer this cycle — bid/payment bonds and NAICS 237990 construction posture are the gate. Either way, attend the site visit and get on W912QR's radar; Nolin River and Rough River lakes (same district, same distance) recycle this requirement.
