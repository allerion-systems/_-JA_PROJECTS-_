## Category 1 — Roof + Fall-Protection Safety Gear and PPE

The strongest dropship category. Three programs with published, verifiable terms; all parcel-shippable.

### 1.1 Texas America Safety Co. (TASCO) — Brownwood, TX — **GO**
- **Carries:** hard hats, safety glasses, hi-vis vests, gloves, hearing protection, general PPE.
- **Program:** Wholesaling / reseller drop-ship program.
- **Requirements:** be a reseller in good standing; resale certificate (fax 1-325-646-3790 or email sales@tasco-safety.com); Net30 credit application or credit card. **No minimum orders, no dropship fees.**
- **Ships direct-to-customer:** yes — approved resellers queue drop-ship orders from their site. Blind/neutral packaging **unconfirmed — ask on the call.**
- **Freight:** parcel; free shipping on $300+ orders ($1,000+ for gloves/rainwear).
- **Territory:** none published.
- **Verified:** https://www.tasco-safety.com/pages/company-information/wholesaling-information.html (fetched 2026-09-06). Phone 1-800-646-5346.

### 1.2 Advanced Safety Devices (ASD) — Los Angeles, CA — **GO (only published true blind-ship)**
- **Carries:** ear/head protection, gloves, headlamps; partial catalog fit — cherry-pick PPE lines.
- **Program:** ASD Drop Ship Program (Dropship Application + signed ASD Dealer Agreement).
- **Requirements:** completed application + dealer agreement; dealer pricing disclosed only after approval (**terms gated behind application**). **No dropship fee**, no minimums published.
- **Ships direct-to-customer:** **yes, explicitly blind** — ships "blindly" to our customer or inserts *our* invoice; approval grants licensed use of their product images/text.
- **Freight:** parcel, "no double freight cost."
- **Verified:** https://www.safety-devices.com/dropship-reseller-wholesale.php (fetched 2026-09-06).

### 1.3 FrenchCreek Fall Safety — Franklin, PA — **GO (fall-protection anchor)**
- **Carries:** harnesses, lanyards, SRLs, anchors, guardrail/ladder safety, rescue — the exact roof-safety catalog.
- **Program:** Distributor program (online application → welcome letter on approval).
- **Requirements:** submit distributor application form; no eligibility hurdles published. **No minimum order requirements. No MAP requirements.**
- **Ships direct-to-customer:** implied by freight language (single shipment to one U.S. destination); **blind-ship unconfirmed — ask.**
- **Freight:** free on $1,500+ single shipments to one U.S. destination (oversize/AK/HI excepted); otherwise parcel/LTL by order size. They supply CSV product data + images to distributors (licensed use).
- **Verified:** https://fcsafety.com/how-to-become-safety-equipment-distributor/ (fetched 2026-09-06). Phone 814-437-1808.

### 1.4 Radians — Memphis, TN — INVESTIGATE (brand depth, regional)
- **Program:** "Become a Distributor" application; **terms gated behind application**; dropship capability unconfirmed.
- **Verified:** https://www.radians.com/become-a-distributor (search-verified 2026-09-05 memo). Phone 877-723-4267.

## Zero-Capital Sequencing — Apply-First Three

Criteria: no inventory commitment, no dealer-lot requirement, parcel-shippable, published (not just implied) program.

1. **TASCO** — no minimums, no fees, published reseller dropship, parcel PPE = sellable SKUs within days of resale-certificate fax. Fastest revenue-capable program on this list.
2. **ASD Drop Ship Program** — the only supplier with *published blind* shipping and licensed image/copy use on approval; zero fees. Solves the branding problem the others leave open.
3. **FrenchCreek** — no minimums, **no MAP**, online application, CSV product data + images supplied, free freight at $1,500+ to one address (built for jobsite drops). Gives us a real fall-protection catalog — the category closest to our roofing customer.

Fast-follow (week 2): Tommy Docks dealer application (docks) and Orgill dealer account (consumables engine — heavier onboarding, biggest catalog payoff).

### Kentucky paperwork MVS must have in hand BEFORE applying
- [ ] **EIN** (IRS, for the LLC) — every credit/dealer application asks for it.
- [ ] **KY Sales and Use Tax Permit** (account number) — register via Kentucky Business One Stop / Kentucky Online Gateway (KOG) account. Prerequisite for the resale certificate.
- [ ] **Completed KY Resale Certificate — Form 51A105** — carries the Sales & Use Tax account number; this is the document TASCO wants faxed and every wholesaler will file. (Verified: https://www.taxformfinder.org/kentucky/form-51a105, search-verified 2026-09-06.)
- [ ] **W-9** prepared for the LLC (name/EIN/address) — requested at account setup by most distributors.
- [ ] Business address, phone, and live website URL (applications ask where product will be sold).
- Optional but door-opening: a one-page line sheet on who MVS is (3D design center, KY contractor customer base).

## Category 7 — Metal carports & garages (manufacturer dealer programs)

The metal-building industry sells almost entirely through dealer networks — the big
configurator sites are themselves dealers, not manufacturers. That makes this the most
natural dealer lane MVS has: the manufacturer fabricates AND INSTALLS on the customer's
pad; the dealer configures, quotes, and collects the deposit.

- **Eagle Carports** (Mount Airy NC, founded 1997, 31 states, 6 plants): published
  dealer program — application asks for legal US business entity, business type, and
  prior manufacturer/dealer history; 20-year paint guarantee. Verified:
  eaglecarports.com/blog/how-to-become-a-carport-dealer. KY is in its service area —
  confirm territory on application.
- **Carolina Carports / American Steel Carports**: dealer programs exist by industry
  structure; application details not published openly — phone/application-gated. Mark:
  verify before listing.
- Fit with the new Garages & Carports designer: our configurator produces the exact
  option sheet these manufacturers' order forms use (roof style, gauge, leg height,
  enclosure, doors, certification) — the designer output IS the dealer order.
- Deposit-model caution: carport dealers typically collect 10-17% deposits with balance
  due at install — matches our authorize-then-capture rule; never take full payment up
  front for a manufacturer-installed unit.
