# R&B Roofing and Remodeling — Design Consultation Templates

Blank, reusable client-facing documents for design-consultation leads:

- **`TEMPLATE_Design-Consultation-Intake-Form.docx`** — client intake & site-visit form. Fill in client/property details, check the requested services, and record observations during the walk-through. Both parties sign the acknowledgment; it explicitly does not create a construction contract.
- **`TEMPLATE_Design-Consultation-Contingency-Agreement.docx`** — consultation agreement covering scope of services, consultation fee (with 100% credit toward a construction contract signed within 90 days), no-construction-obligation contingencies, an optional insurance contingency, design-material ownership, and the Kentucky 3-business-day right to cancel.

## Usage

1. Copy the template, fill in the client name, property address, date, fee, and proposal turnaround before the visit.
2. Print both for wet signature at the site visit (or send for e-signature).
3. Keep executed copies in the job file — **never commit filled-in client copies to this repo**; they contain personal information.
