combined with the 1-1/2" polyiso base") with the 8/24 call ("four inches of ISO"): 4.5" at the low
point, more everywhere else.

## Notes driving the RFQ

- **Item 3 — stock on hand.** 7/29 site count found **64 boards of 1.5" polyiso** (2 bundles × 32)
  and TPO reported in stock. Two flat layers need **134 boards**; 64 on hand → buy 70. RFQ asks
  for both the 70-board price and the full 134-board price so we can go either way once the yard
  is physically counted. **Count it before ordering.**
- **Item 6 — fastener length.** The 8/24 call said 5". Over 4.5" of insulation on steel deck, a 5"
  fastener leaves nothing past the flute. Both suppliers are asked to confirm against the actual
  assembly; we calculate 6". Getting this wrong voids the uplift rating.
- **Item 4 — tapered.** The single biggest variable and the reason both quotes must include a
  layout drawing, a bill of materials and an R-value calc at the thinnest point — not a lump sum.
- **Item 8 — adhesive.** Assumes the parapet flashing is adhered. If the wall substrate takes
  mechanically-fastened membrane with intermediate bars, this drops by roughly $700. Confirm the
  wall construction on the next site visit.
- **Warranty.** Not in the sold estimate. Both suppliers are asked to price 15- and 20-year NDL
  separately, **and to state the installer certification required** — Elias's crew is brand new
  and may not carry it.
