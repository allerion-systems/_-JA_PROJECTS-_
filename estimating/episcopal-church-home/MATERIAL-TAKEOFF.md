## 1. CODE ANSWER — R-25ci, and the estimate is already right

| | |
|---|---|
| Jurisdiction | Louisville / Jefferson County, KY |
| Climate zone | **4A** (mixed-humid) |
| Adopted commercial energy code | **2012 Kentucky Energy Conservation Code** (2012 IECC w/ KY amendments) — used with the Kentucky Building Code for all occupancies **except** R-2/R-3/R-4 |
| Table C402.2, Roofs — insulation entirely above deck, CZ4 | **R-25ci** |
| Applies to this job? | **Yes.** C503.3.1: a roof *replacement* must meet the new-construction R-value where the assembly has insulation entirely above deck. This is a full tear-off to deck. |
| Can we go lower? | **No.** Code also states the R-value shall not be reduced as part of a roof replacement. |

**The R-25 in the Handoff estimate is the correct code number. Nobody has to go back to Beverly.**
Kentucky is still on the 2012-based code — it did **not** follow the 2015+ IECC jump to R-30 that
neighboring states took. If someone quotes you R-30, they are reading the wrong edition.

> Occupancy caveat: if Roof A covers **independent-living apartments (R-2)**, the *2009* code applies
> instead and the requirement is lower. R-25 satisfies both, so build to R-25 and don't reopen it.

### How the tapered layer counts — this is the trap

Under the **2012** IECC (what Kentucky enforces), §C402.2.1 Exception allows an area-weighted
U-factor only **where insulation thickness varies 1 inch or less**. You do *not* get to average a
deep taper. Compliance is judged essentially **at the thin point, plus at most 1 inch of taper**.

The 2021 IECC changed this to an average-thickness method — **that does not apply here.** Anyone
who tells you "the taper averages out to R-25" is quoting the wrong code edition.

## 2. ⚠️ Fastener length — the 5" number is wrong

The 8/24 call said 5" fasteners. That only works on a 4" flat stack. This assembly is 4.5" at the
low point and roughly **9.6" at the parapets** once the taper builds. Mechanically-attached membrane
fasteners must pass through everything and get ~3/4" past the deck flute:

| Location | Assembly | Minimum fastener |
|---|---|---|
| Low point / drains | 4.5" | **5.5"** |
| Mid-field | ~6.0" | **7.0"** |
| High point at parapet | ~9.6" | **10.5"** |

**Multiple lengths required.** Both RFQs ask the supplier to schedule quantities per length off
their own tapered layout. Ordering one length is how you end up short on the roof.

## 3. ⚠️ The build-up is 4.5"–9.6". What does it collide with?

This is a tear-off going back thicker than what came off. Before ordering, walk the roof and check:

- **Equipment curbs (2, 32 LF).** Estimate carries curb *flashing* only — no curb extensions. A 6–9"
  build-up may bury them. Raising curbs is a change order.
- **Roof drains (3).** New bowls are in the estimate; confirm the sump depth works with 4.5" at the
  low point and that the leaders can be re-set.
- **Pipe penetrations (6) and exhaust caps (2).** May need extending.
- **Door thresholds / roof hatch / access.** Any at-grade transition loses up to 9" of clearance.
- **Parapet heights.** The 5' walls become ~4'2" at the high side. Still fine for the flashing detail,
  but confirm the term bar lands on solid substrate.

**Mark drain locations while you're up there** — the supplier cannot design the taper without them.

## 6. THE TAPERED PACKAGE — biggest variable on the job

Assumed layout (supplier's design governs): center valley down the 65' length, 3 drains in the
valley, cross-slope 15' each side from the long parapets, 1.0" minimum at the drains.

| Slope | Avg thickness | Volume | Max thickness | @$1.20 | @$1.35 | @$1.60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1/4":12** cross, 1/8":12 valley | 3.56" | **7,472 bd-ft** | 6.12" | $8,966 | $10,087 | $11,955 |
| **1/8":12** cross, 1/16":12 valley | 2.28" | **4,786 bd-ft** | 3.56" | $5,743 | $6,461 | $7,658 |

**The slope decision is worth about $3,600 — and roughly 5 points of margin.** IBC wants 1/4":12 design slope on a new low-slope
membrane, and reroofing generally has to comply — with an independent inspector reviewing this job,
**carry 1/4":12 as the base bid.** Price 1/8":12 as an alternate and only take it with the AHJ's
concurrence in writing. Do not quietly build it flatter.

## 7. What has to happen before anyone orders

1. **Count the yard.** 7/29 logged 64 boards of 1.5" polyiso and "TPO in stock." Confirmed stock is
   worth **$1,400–$5,600** straight to margin.
2. **Mark the 3 drain locations** and measure to the walls. No taper design without it.
2b. **Re-walk the field dimension.** Aerial says 19.0 SQ, field measure says 21 SQ. Settle it.
2c. **Drainage tributary area.** The surrounding pitched shingle roofs discharge into this
    well — the drains serve far more than the 2,100 SF of flat roof. Ask the supplier whether
    3 drains is adequate; overflow/scupper provisions may be required.
2d. **Rooftop equipment.** Aerial shows one large RTU (~9 condenser fans) on rails, a second
    long unit with 2 fans, plus a curb-mounted box and ductwork. The estimate carries only
    2 curbs / 32 LF. Raising or re-supporting that equipment over a 4.5–9.6" build-up is
    **not in the sold scope** — price it before it becomes a field argument.
3. **Open the three quote PDFs in §4** and replace the two polyiso placeholders.
4. **Confirm deck gauge** — 20-ga assumed from the Gustavo's spec; the 8/24 call only said "metal B-deck."
5. **Decide the warranty.** No NDL is priced in the sold scope, and NDL requires a certified
   installer — which bears directly on putting Elias's brand-new crew on it.
