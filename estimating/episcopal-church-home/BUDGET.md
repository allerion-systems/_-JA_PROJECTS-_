## 3. ⚠️ The one thing to settle with Elias before he signs

**$350/SQ against *what* squares.** This is the exact fight that is still open on Texas Roadhouse
("missing thirty-five squares," 72 vs 78 vs 121). Do not repeat it here.

| Basis | Squares | Crew pay | What it really works out to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Field only | 21.00 | $7,350 | $1.87/SF of membrane actually installed |
| Field + 15% waste | 24.15 | $8,453 | $2.15/SF |
| **Field + walls (all membrane)** | **39.40** | **$13,790** | **$3.50/SF** ✅ |
| All membrane + 15% waste | 45.31 | $15,859 | $4.03/SF |

The wall flashing is 1,840 SF — **47% of all the membrane on this job**, and it is 10' tall on
every side, which is the slowest, most detail-heavy work on the roof. No crew tears off, cants,
flashes, bars and welds 184 LF of 10' wall for free. If the subcontract says "$350/SQ" against
21 SQ, Elias's crew walks or you eat a change order.

**Write it as a lump sum, not a rate:**

> *"Demolition and installation labor, complete: **$13,790.00 lump sum**, based on 39.40 squares of
> installed TPO membrane (21.00 SQ field + 18.40 SQ wall flashing, 184 LF × 10') at $350.00 per
> square. Quantity is fixed and not subject to re-measure. Deck replacement is excluded and by
> change order only."*

## 4. Recommended structure: R&B furnishes material, Elias is labor-only

Three reasons this beats letting Elias buy the material:

1. **Scott has 60-day terms with QXO on commercial.** R&B buys the material and floats zero cash —
   ERS pays before the material bill is due. Elias has no such terms; he'd need deposits.
2. **Elias's crew is brand new** (started this week). Do not hand a new crew a $21K material buy
   and a supplier-designed tapered package on their first job.
3. **We keep the material margin and control the spec** — critical if a manufacturer warranty is
   required (see risks).

## 9. Payment terms to write into Elias's subcontract

His crew is new and has no float. Do not put him on "paid when paid" for the whole job.

- 3 draws: **35% at tear-off + dry-in / 45% at membrane and flashing complete / 20% at final punch + owner sign-off**
- No deposit. Material is R&B's, so he has no buy-in to fund.
- Retainage 5% max, released at final — or waive it and hold the 20% final draw instead.
- Certificate of insurance naming **R&B Roofing & Remodeling as additional insured** in hand
  *before* mobilization. (Same trap as the Wawa/framing insurance hold-up.)
- Deck replacement, interior protection, and anything behind the tear-off note is **change order only**.
