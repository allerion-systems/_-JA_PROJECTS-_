# Day-Of Run Sheet — Template

Times below assume a **5:30pm ceremony with a first look**; shift everything to your real ceremony time by working *backward* from it. Every vendor gets this sheet the week of, and your point person holds the master copy with full authority to adjust on the fly.

> **Buffer rule:** the schedule fails at hair/makeup or photos, never at dinner. Pad the front half, not the back.

| Time | What | Who owns it |
|------|------|-------------|
| 8:30 | Hair & makeup starts (order: largest/most services first; couple **last**) | HMU lead |
| 12:00 | Lunch delivered to both getting-ready locations (real food, not chips) | Point person |
| 13:30 | Photographer + video arrive; detail shots (rings, invites, attire) | Photographer |
| 14:00 | Couple dressed; HMU **hard stop** (45-min buffer built in behind this) | HMU lead |
| 14:30 | First look + couple portraits | Photographer |
| 15:15 | Wedding party photos | Photographer |
| 16:00 | Family formals (grouping list printed; a designated family wrangler per side) | Photographer + wranglers |
| 16:45 | Photos done; couple tucked away out of sight; touch-ups | Point person |
| 16:45 | Vendors final check: ceremony seating (160 chairs), sound, florals, programs | Coordinator |
| 17:00 | Guest arrival opens; prelude music; ushers/greeters in position | Ushers |
| 17:15 | Shuttle 1 & 2 arrive (if running); family seated last | Shuttle captain |
| 17:30 | **Ceremony** (processional → vows → rings → kiss; 25–30 min) | Officiant |
| 18:00 | Cocktail hour opens (2–3 bartenders on); couple: 5 minutes alone first | Bar lead |
| 18:05 | Any remaining family formals (keep under 20 min) | Photographer |
| 18:30 | Couple joins cocktail hour OR rests — decide in advance | Couple |
| 18:50 | Guests invited to find tables (table chart + escort cards visible from cocktail area) | Coordinator |
| 19:05 | Wedding party + couple entrance | DJ/band |
| 19:10 | First dance (while everyone's already watching) | DJ/band |
| 19:15 | Welcome/blessing (2 min max) → first course / buffet opens (2-sided, 2 stations for 154) | Caterer |
| 19:20 | Couple eats the held plates. Non-negotiable. | Caterer |
| 19:45 | Couple table visits begin (~20 tables × 2–3 min through end of dinner) | Couple |
| 20:15 | Toasts — 3 max, 3–5 min each, during dessert lull | DJ/band emcee |
| 20:35 | Parent dances | DJ/band |
| 20:45 | Dance floor opens | DJ/band |
| 21:30 | Cake cutting (photographer flagged 5 min prior) | Caterer |
| 22:00 | Last shuttle-run announcement if applicable | DJ/band |
| 22:30 | Bar last call (closes 30 min before end) | Bar lead |
| 22:50 | Last dance (private last dance optional: everyone out, just you two + empty room) | DJ/band |
| 23:00 | Send-off; gifts/cards secured by designated person; couple departs | Point person |
| 23:00–24:00 | Vendor breakdown; rentals staged; venue walkthrough + sign-out | Coordinator |

## Attach to this sheet

- Vendor list with cell numbers + arrival times
- Family formals grouping list (aim ≤ 12 groupings; ~3–4 min each)
- Seating chart + floor plan
- Payment/tip envelope manifest
- Rain call (if outdoor): decision time, decision owner, and what moves where
