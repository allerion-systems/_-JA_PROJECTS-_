| Projection at End 2 | 36″ |

## What the numbers say

- **Projection grows uniformly** from 35 3/4″ at End 1 to 36″ at End 2 —
  +1/4″ over the 23′-1″ run. The mid-span reading of 35 7/8″ is exactly the
  straight-line interpolation, so the fascia is **straight, not bowed** — it's
  uniformly out of parallel with the building face.
- **The fascia edge is 3/16″ longer than the wall edge** (277 3/16″ vs 277″).
  Corner-to-corner length computed from the perpendicular projections alone
  would be ≈277″, so the extra 3/16″ means the **end returns are not
  perpendicular** to the building face. Pull **both diagonals** to fully close
  the geometry before fabricating anything.

## Framing implications

1. Cut outlookers/rafters to the run — lengths vary 35 3/4″ → 36″ along the
   277″ length. Don't gang-cut to one dimension.
2. Field-measure each end closure individually; neither end is a square cut.
3. If the fascia needs to *read* parallel to the building, decide up front:
   split the difference (hold ~35 7/8″ and scribe) or follow the as-built line.
